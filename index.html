Meadow Night

草原之夜

A quiet meadow by a lake: fireflies, snowy peaks, aurora and the Moon. Walk, swim, and dive into a hidden underwater garden of kelp, corals and a shipwreck.

湖边一片安静的草原：萤火虫、雪山、极光和月亮。散步、游泳，或潜入水潭，在海藻、珊瑚和沉船之间探索湖底花园。

ABOUT

关于

Made by Science Sheep — a solo developer and science writer, building this world one chapter at a time. Every chapter tries to be two things at once: a place worth visiting, and a place where you learn something true.

由 科学羊 一个人制作——一名独立开发者，也是科普作者，一章一章地把这个世界做下去。每一章都想同时做到两件事：值得去看看，也能让你学到真东西。

Found a bug, a wrong fact, or have an idea? I read everything.

发现了问题、数据写错了，或者有新想法？每一条我都会看。
